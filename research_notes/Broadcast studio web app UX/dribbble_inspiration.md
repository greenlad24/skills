# Dribbble UI inspiration: Sunrise Jam live-show studio (broadcast + mixing + AI process sidebar)

How this was done (2026-10-16): I ran 26 Dribbble searches through a real-browser helper, ranked results by the likes/views shown on each card, then opened 21 shots and looked at them. For video shots I pulled frames from the MP4 with ffmpeg. Screenshots are in `/home/user/skills/research_notes/Broadcast studio web app UX/dribbble/`. Likes/views are the card numbers at capture time. The hex values marked **[DP]** come from the "Download color palette" swatches that Dribbble extracts from each shot. They describe the image, so they are not the designer's stated tokens. Hex values marked **[est.]** are my visual estimates.

Popularity rule from the brief: a pattern counts as **validated** if it appears in a shot with at least 100 likes or at least 10k views, or if it recurs across at least 2 different designers.

## Q1. Broadcast studio layout: program/preview multiview, camera tiles, go-live, break screen

### Takeaway
The validated layout is a **big program monitor top-center with controls in a bottom band and a right column for live side-info**. "LIVE" is always a **red pill badge overlaid on the tile corner**. I found no high-engagement Dribbble shot that shows true broadcast program/preview (red/green tally) or a break-screen countdown. That convention has to come from pro tools (vMix/ATEM/OBS), not from Dribbble.

### Cited Findings
- The OBS concept puts one large program view at roughly 55% width top-center, stream info (title, category, "Go live note", tags, SAVE) in a left column, and chat in a right column. Under the video is a bottom band of labelled panels: SCREENS (scene thumbnails + "Main Screen"), SOURCES (Webcam, Playing Now with eye toggles), MIXER (two ring-knobs "HD Audio" and "Mic\AUX"), SCENE TRANSITIONS (Fade · 300 ms dropdowns), CONTROLS (round red record button in the center), Stream Stats (Session 02:56, Followers, Likes, Views) and system STATS tiles (GPU 14%, Mem 23%, CPU 31%) — [uixNinja, 537 likes / 203k views](https://dribbble.com/shots/22597564-OBS-Studio-concept-with-AI-and-happy-song)
- An "Offline" status card with a lightning icon floats out as a state indicator — [uixNinja](https://dribbble.com/shots/22597564-OBS-Studio-concept-with-AI-and-happy-song)
- Canvas-first streaming UI: the video canvas is outlined in white on a near-black background. One bottom floating pill toolbar holds tools, mic, resolution (1920×1080) and "Start Virtual Webcam". A thin left rail of numbered slots (1–10) handles scenes, and Avatar/Scenes buttons sit bottom-right. A resolution popover warns "May impact performance." Palette [DP]: #050506, #403A43, #C78463, #BF4C2F, #A66D54, #DDDCDB, #767F8B — [Erik Padamans, 301 / 68.9k](https://dribbble.com/shots/26554251-Hyper-Desktop-Vtube-App-Streaming-UI)
- The Hyper palette is warm: terracotta #BF4C2F and copper #C78463 on #050506. That is a near-exact match for a "sunrise on black" brand — [Erik Padamans](https://dribbble.com/shots/26554251-Hyper-Desktop-Vtube-App-Streaming-UI)
- Multiview: several live event panels sit in columns. Each has a red "LIVE" pill between the competitors, a close (×) icon, a score grid, and an "Add live event" header slot. The left nav is a collapsed icon rail with small labels and red notification dots. Accent palette [DP]: #040404, #454B4B, #EBF802 (electric yellow), #A4A10F — [Flatstudio, 141 / 38.1k](https://dribbble.com/shots/15978860-Parimatch-Multi-view)
- The designer's rationale for multiview: "In gambling, each second counts. When in live, people tend to follow events… they need them constantly on their [screen]" — [Flatstudio](https://dribbble.com/shots/15978860-Parimatch-Multi-view)
- The red "Live" pill and the "eye + 1.2K" viewer pill are overlaid top-left/top-right on every video thumbnail, and the follow button is a solid blue pill — [Vektora, 522 / 166k](https://dribbble.com/shots/18627261-GoLive-Streaming-App)
- Camera-tile health/status: frosted chips across the top of the feed ("Record an audio", "Record a video", "Turn off the audio", "Turn off the camera"). A detection pill appears with an icon ("Animal detected") along with a blue corner-bracket focus box. Below sit an event timeline scrubber with tick marks and an Activities tab with a red count badge. Palette [DP]: #0D0D0B, #625F5C, #2E422F, #453A30, #8E979C, #DADFDF — [Phenomenon Studio, 191 / 38.8k](https://dribbble.com/shots/26952820-Live-Camera-Monitoring-Mobile-UI-Sentry)
- Studio preview with a transport bar and a "2160P · 120 FPS" format readout in the monitor header, plus a "Saved" cloud chip. Vertical green→yellow stereo meters are pinned at the right edge of the timeline — [Vladyslav Kara, 130 / 45.9k](https://dribbble.com/shots/22724098-Video-Audio-Studio-Suite-Recofy-Dashboard)

### Inferences
- **Validated:** big-monitor-plus-bottom-control-band (OBS concept is high engagement; Recofy uses the same monitor-over-timeline idea, so 2 designers). Red "LIVE" pill on the tile corner (GoLive, Parimatch, also the red Rec dot in Layo, so 3+ designers). Floating pill toolbar for a minimal mode (Hyper, high views).
- For Sunrise Jam: Program = large tile with a red border and a "LIVE" pill. Preview = a smaller tile beside it with a green border. Below them sits a row of camera tiles (16:9, 12px radius) whose corner badges reuse the Sentry chip style: signal/fps/battery/audio dots. "Go Live" should be the single solid warm-red/orange pill in the header (Hyper's "Start Virtual Webcam" logic: one primary action). The break screen can reuse the OBS concept's huge display typography ("Hot News" style, ~64px bold) for a countdown.
- Dribbble's OBS concept spends space on chat, while a band show needs camera health more. Replace the chat column with the AI/process sidebar (Q3).

### Gaps
- No Dribbble shot found (in 26 searches including "video switcher", "multiview", "control room", "broadcast dashboard") with real broadcast program/preview tally colouring or a break/"be right back" countdown screen. The "video switcher" and "control room" searches returned unrelated toggles and 3D dioramas. These conventions need to be sourced from vendor UIs instead.
- uixNinja's OBS shot has no extracted palette (video-led shot). Visually it is a near-black purple base (~#1A1A24 [est.]), lavender accent (~#A78BFA [est.]) and a red record dot.

## Q2. Audio mixing station: channel strips, faders, meters, EQ, dynamics, knobs

### Takeaway
High-engagement audio shots use **modular columns with a colour identity per module**, **arc-ring knobs with a coloured value arc**, **segmented LED meters (green→yellow→red, or teal-in / red-out)**, and **one big macro knob** for the non-expert. X-Vox (397/117k) is the best template for "simple surface, pro depth".

### Cited Findings
- X-Vox: four module columns (DYNAMICS pink, TONE cyan, SPACE purple, SFX yellow). Each has a power toggle (top-left), Solo "S" (top-right), a neon line icon, a preset name ("TUBE + FET", "CLEAR", "ROOM", "PITCH WIDENER") and an output trim. Lower area: GR/DS vertical meters next to COMP 50% / DE-ESS 50% knobs, an EQ curve display (±12 dB) over Low/Mid/High/Air+ mini-faders, Reverb/Delay meters with IN/OUT scales (−∞ to +12), and a huge "WIDE · 50%" knob with a dotted value ring. A global INPUT/OUTPUT LED meter strip with "+0.0 dB" readouts runs along the footer, with Undo/Redo, a preset browser and BYPASS in the header — [Voicu Apostol, 397 / 117k](https://dribbble.com/shots/20314717-Neumorphic-Audio-Plugin)
- Ofspace plugin: a spectrum split into 4 coloured frequency bands (orange, pink, purple, blue), each with a floating numeric value tag, a two-state character toggle ("thick/punch", "gentle/warm", "flipped/direct", "excite/smooth") and S/M buttons. Tall in/out meters (teal in, red/orange out) carry a dB value flag ("-0.7 Db", "24 Db") on a 0 to −56 scale. The central knob has multi-colour arcs. Palette [DP]: #131316, #274C5D, #571C1C, #172051, #C63531, #2552C2, #1B91F6, #CE3FB1 — [Ofspace, 329 / 58.8k](https://dribbble.com/shots/6138741-Audio-Plugin)
- Layo EQ dashboard: knobs with tick marks and a small accent marker (BASS LEVEL, OUTPUT LEVEL). NOISE CONTROLS is a 3-node segmented Off/On/Noise-aware selector, followed by a "Voice detect" toggle. EQUALIZER pairs a ±10 scale with a "3D surround" preset dropdown. A "• Connected" device row, a round jog transport, separate STOP and red REC/PAUSE circles, and L/R sliders with "-4dB"/"-12dB" readouts complete it. Periwinkle accent (~#7B8CF7 [est.]) and a yellow waveform playhead on ~#111 [est.] — [Layo, 526 / 142k](https://dribbble.com/shots/19695957-Audio-equalizer-dashboard-concept)
- Custom DAW mixer row: each channel strip has a name, an "Inserts" dropdown, a Pan knob, Send A/Send B knobs, a tall green segmented meter with a dB scale (6 to −64), FX/S/R buttons and a red-filled R when armed, with a black "Master" strip at the far right. Track colours (blue, purple, green, pink) match the arrange view. Palette [DP]: #09090C, #1336FE, #3B344C, #CC2C9D, #3BA160, #B893AE — [Carlos R Andujar, 72 / 13.5k](https://dribbble.com/shots/4960678-Custom-DAW)
- The designer of Custom DAW tested "an idea on color in music software UI so I designed 2 versions, a dark UI" (and light) — [Carlos R Andujar](https://dribbble.com/shots/4960678-Custom-DAW)
- Simple creator mixer: an app icon above each fader serves as the channel label, with a % readout under each fader and a green glowing line at the fader cap. The L/R segmented meter (−42 to 0) uses white segments. Also: a monospace timecode "00:24:25", a "123.00 kHz · mp3" LCD chip, REC/PAUSE (red dot), STOP and MARK, and MAIN/MIX/INPUT/OUTPUT tabs. Palette [DP]: #040404, #6A6A6A, #D41C15, #1A67CC, #EDEDED — [Serhii Huba, 17 / 6.8k](https://dribbble.com/shots/25785310-Audio-Mixer-Interface-for-B2C-Creator-SaaS)
- Fusion 002 multiband saturator: three band columns with crossover-frequency pills (400HZ, 1.5KHZ) between them. A large central DRIVE knob is flanked by GAIN and TONE, with a per-band headphone (solo) button. Each band has a colour "disc" identity, one of them a pink→orange sunrise gradient. A "SOFTCLIP" chip and a power icon sit in the header — [Julian Behrens, 111 / 17.2k](https://dribbble.com/shots/24845241-Multiband-Saturator-VST-Plugin-GUI)
- An XY pad as a macro: WIDTH 67% × ENERGY 71% on a dotted grid with a glowing puck, plus a SOFT/MEDIUM/HARD segmented control and a single OUTPUT knob — [Timothy Boros, 344 / 48.6k](https://dribbble.com/shots/22677931-KNORR-VST-Plugin-Concept)
- The hardware language of black body plus a single yellow accent: knurled knob, tape-reel display, mono labels ("tape preview") — [Riotters, 344 / 79.7k](https://dribbble.com/shots/24463314-Rtt-01-music-plugin)

### Inferences
- **Validated:** arc/ring knobs with a value arc (Ofspace, X-Vox, Layo, OBS concept: 4 designers). Segmented LED meters (X-Vox, Ofspace, Custom DAW, Huba: 4 designers). Colour per module/channel (X-Vox, Ofspace, Custom DAW, Fusion: 4 designers). One big macro knob for the non-expert (X-Vox "WIDE", KNORR XY, Fusion "DRIVE": 3 designers). Numeric readout attached to every control (all audio shots).
- Proposed Sunrise Jam mixer: "Simple" view = one strip per band member (Vocals, Keys, Bass, Guitar, Drums, Horns), each with an instrument icon as the label à la Huba, a big fader, a dB readout, a segmented meter, Mute/Solo, and one "Tone" macro knob. "Pro" drawer per strip = X-Vox-style module tabs (EQ curve with 4 bands, Dynamics with a GR meter, Space sends), with colour identity per module. Use warm meter colours (green→amber→red is still required for clipping semantics) and the Fusion sunrise gradient only for brand moments such as the master strip, never for meter states.
- Neumorphic/skeuomorphic light surfaces (X-Vox lower half, Fusion, KNORR) are engagement-rich but glare in a dark venue. Keep the depth cues (inner shadow on knobs, raised caps) but render them on dark surfaces.

### Gaps
- Searches for "mixing console" returned almost nothing above 100 likes (top result 111/17.2k, a plugin). Dribbble has very few full multi-channel live-console UIs with high engagement. Custom DAW (72/13.5k) and Huba (17/6.8k) are the only literal strip rows, so the strip anatomy is validated by recurrence, not by popularity.
- No shot showed a compressor transfer curve or gate visual. Dynamics are only shown as GR meters plus knobs.

## Q3. Collapsible process/AI sidebar: icon rail vs expanded, activity feed

### Takeaway
The validated pattern is a **two-level sidebar**: a 48–56px icon rail that stays visible, plus a ~240px labelled panel that collapses. AI and background work show up as a **"Live Activity" stream** (name · id/time · right-aligned duration or status chip) and, on drill-in, a **stepper or Gantt execution timeline** with collapsible steps.

### Cited Findings
- Live Activity column: a green live dot with "Live Activity", plus a pause button and a Filter button. Each row shows the agent name (bold), "r_8a3f12 • 14:32:18" in a smaller grey second line, and right-aligned "1.4s / 12.4k tok". Exceptions replace the metric with a chip: "Retry 1/3" (amber) or "Tool Error" (red text). KPI cards with sparklines sit at the top (Runs 12,438; Success 98.7%; Tokens 8.4M), and a "Live monitoring across 24 agents" subtitle. Palette [DP]: #020202, #A3A3AA, #134924, #5F5F68, #078A31 (green), #D73C10 (red-orange), #FAFBFB, #3E60B2 — [Yuri Gokoryan, 133 / 29.3k](https://dribbble.com/shots/27408472-AI-Agent-Observability-Platform-UI-Design)
- Run detail: "Execution Timeline · 9 steps · 9710ms" shown as rows with icon, step name, a one-line monospace detail, a horizontal Gantt bar (green, amber for retry), duration and tokens. Sub-steps are nested and indented. A "Completed" green chip sits beside the run id. "Show tool I/O" and "Raw trace" toggles appear, and a right "Decisions" panel shows the considered strategies with scores and the "Selected Reasoning" — [Yuri Gokoryan](https://dribbble.com/shots/27408472-AI-Agent-Observability-Platform-UI-Design)
