## Q1. Which products do reviewers repeatedly call easiest, and why (specific UI elements)?

### Takeaway
Reviewers name browser studios (StreamYard, Restream Studio, Riverside, and Evmux) as the easiest for desktop streaming. They name tap-to-switch tablet apps (Mevo Multicam, Switcher Studio, Cinamaker) as the easiest for multicam. Prism is "easy OBS" and Ecamm is "easy pro". OBS and vMix are powerful but take effort to learn. The ease comes from a small set of repeated UI patterns:
- one big "stage" preview with a "backstage" tray of sources underneath
- one-click layout buttons
- a right sidebar of tabs for brand, banners, comments and chat
- guests joining from a link with no account
- a single, confirmed Go Live button

### Cited Findings
**Who is called easiest**
- Think Media opens its tutorial with "if you're looking for an easy way to go live... stream yard is the way to go." The studio is described as "your home base," and it has one blue "Go Live" button. [V: 135k] — [SY-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDx8fKnU3Sk)
- Feyrer Jr. compared Restream Studio and StreamYard on time-to-live. In both, you click the gear, pick camera, mic and speakers, then hit Go Live in the top right. He calls StreamYard's "simplicity... really really nice" but says Restream now has more layouts and better layout transitions for free. [C: 1; 34k] — [SYvRS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2VbjcZaB4TM). Chris with Minn Media agrees on Restream: "the dashboard is about as simple as they come... click on new stream and it essentially just walks you right into the studio." [C: 2 channels agree that browser studios are near-zero setup] — [RS-Minn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNn6HbDIrlE)
- Primal Video ranks browser-based Evmux first for beginners, then Prism, then OBS for intermediate-to-advanced users, then vMix as most advanced. It says browser studios "all share a very similar interface": connect camera and mic, screen share, bring in guests, and switch with preset templates. On Mac it ranks Ecamm Live as "the best allrounder", the "sweet spot" between Evmux-easy and OBS-control. [C: 1 channel, 2 videos, 56k and 32k] — [PRIMAL-PC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0h0wOS_EfJI), [PRIMAL-MAC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ex_XuySNUzE). Tom Buck's ranking points the same way: "just get ecamm and you'll be happy". He adds that Ecamm is "significantly less picky about what settings you choose", so mixed frame rates "all look great". [C: 2 channels agree Ecamm is easier than OBS] — [ECAMM-v-OBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dxa3BJCyb6Q)
- Prism Live Studio is pitched as an OBS alternative "for some of you [for whom] it may be kind of complicated to use OBS". Its settings include an **"output settings guide"** that applies platform-recommended resolution and bitrate for Twitch, YouTube or Facebook in one click. It recommends **turning Studio Mode off** for beginners: "work with one scene at a time, a little less overwhelming." [V: 276k, sponsored] — [PRISM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LxkmWXR6dVs). Primal Video also calls Prism "very user friendly", with "a little bit more of a learning curve" than Evmux. [C: 2]
- Think Media calls the Mevo multicam system "one of the easiest ways" to make videos: "got it up and running in less than five minutes," and you "literally switch angles" live from a phone or tablet, so there is no editing afterwards. [V: 450k] — [MEVO-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ksEvsSH0oc). TheUnlockr called it "super useful and a clever way" to do wireless multicam with phones you already own. [V: 325k] — [MEVO-Unlockr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPBdWM7Z158)
- Streamlabs was the "straightforward and the easiest to get set up" option a few years ago. Its one-click installable overlay themes are its ease advantage ("OBS requires you to set it up manually piece by piece"). But it now "uses on average a minimum of 8% more CPU than OBS" and is "incredibly buggy" with "constant glitches". [V: 194k] — [SL-v-OBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGHPd7dNjw4)

**The concrete UI elements behind "easy"**
- **Stage plus backstage tray.** In StreamYard your camera and guests sit in a tray under the main preview. You click "Add to stage" or "Remove" to put them on the program. A row of layout buttons under the stage (solo, cropped, side-by-side, spotlight, picture-in-picture, screen-only) changes the composition in one click with an animated transition. [V: 135k; C: 3 — SY-Think, SY-Gauld, SYvRS] — [SY-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDx8fKnU3Sk), [SY-Gauld](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YlcyoE3q7zY). Restream uses the same pattern: guests appear on the left with a **slider toggle to add them to the stream**, and you drag and drop people between layout boxes. [C: 2 — SYvRS, RS-2.0]
- **Right-hand tabbed sidebar** holding Brand (color, theme, logo, overlay, video clips, background, music), Comments, Banners and Private chat. [C: 3 — SY-Think, SY-Gauld, RS-2.0] In Restream Studio 2.0, graphics are "tucked away in the sidebar on the right". **Scenes** are a list on the left that can be reordered by drag and drop, **layouts** sit at the bottom, and "layout changes that you make to a scene will stick." [UNVERIFIED (vendor), 23k] — [RS-2.0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJpj1I2n-Ss)
- **Hover to Show/Hide content**: banners, comments and video clips appear on hover with a "Show" button and animate in and out. [C: 3 — SY-Think, SY-Gauld, RIV-Live]
- **One brand color and theme drives every graphic.** StreamYard has Bubble, Classic, Minimal and Block themes. Riverside's brand palette restyles name labels, lower thirds and on-screen comments, and that styling also applies to guests' lobby pages. [C: 2 — SY-Think, RIV-Live]
- **Tablet multicam: tap a camera thumbnail to make it live.** The top-right tile is program, and you tap any camera tile to switch. This is the same in Mevo Multicam [V: 325k], Switcher Studio ("upper right we have the program window... assets and sources over to the left"; a red outline marks the live source) [V: 142k, vendor], and Cinamaker. [C: 3+] — [MEVO-Unlockr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPBdWM7Z158), [SW-Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MlobdIgwxj0), [CINA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IAHJ5UgYvs4)
- **Graphics typed by purpose rather than layered from scratch.** Mevo offers "full screen overlay, lower third, over the shoulder, corner bug". Switcher has "use as image / corner bug / dynamic templates" with a social-handle lower-third template. You tap once to toggle a graphic on or off. [V: 117k; C: 3 — MEVO-PJ, MEVO-Unlockr, SW-Overview]
- **Ecamm's modular dockable panels** (comments, scenes, audio, camera effects) let users keep only what they need. Tom Buck found this "very confusing at first because everything can just sort of be everywhere," then "super powerful." Ecamm also sets **mic delay in frames rather than milliseconds** ("instead of milliseconds which gets a little confusing"). [UNVERIFIED single channel, 67k] — [ECAMM-v-OBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dxa3BJCyb6Q)

### Inferences
- "Easy" products avoid making the user build a canvas from nothing. They ship with a preset stage and layouts, with typed graphics on top. Scenes and layers stay hidden until the user asks for them.
- A web app can copy the browser-studio layout directly: stage preview in the center, a source tray below it, layout buttons under the stage, a tabbed right panel and one Go Live button. For the band show, the "guests" are phone cameras and the mixer.

### Gaps
- No 100k-plus independent review covered Melon, Cinamaker (20k), Wirecast, or TriCaster/Vizrt. The TriCaster videos found were NewTek vendor videos under 33k views, so TriCaster UX claims are not verified here.
- Restream Studio 2.0 scene behavior has only vendor evidence (23k).
- **Unverified list:** Ecamm modular-panel confusion and mic delay in frames (Tom Buck only). Restream scenes stick per scene (vendor). Evmux has "more customization than StreamYard" (Primal Video only).

## Q2. Common complaints and pain points (confusing scene systems, hidden settings, audio routing)

### Takeaway
The main pain points are:
- an empty canvas plus a scene/source model the user has to understand before anything works (OBS)
- settings mismatches that cause black screens (OBS)
- unintuitive layer and input nesting (vMix)
- important modes hidden in a separate setup app (ATEM)
- audio that silently comes from the wrong device (phone and tablet switchers)
- free-tier stream drops and slow layout changes (Restream)
- hidden CPU cost (Streamlabs, Prism, Studio Mode)

### Cited Findings
- **OBS's empty canvas and scene/source model.** Brett In Tech's 6.3M-view beginner guide says the canvas "should be black" at first, and you must add a source. It skips Scenes as "a little bit more advanced for a beginners tutorial." [V: 6.3M] — [OBS-Brett](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTk99mHDX_I). Primal Video recommends disabling Studio Mode "for simplicity... especially if you're just starting out." [V: 1.6M] — [OBS-Primal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9z9GiEM4uvA). Stream Scheme says "to a beginner it is very daunting to open OBS." [V: 194k] [C: 3 channels]
- **OBS settings mismatches produce black screens.** "Nothing is showing up and it might be because your base canvas resolution is set to the wrong size or your output is set wrong or one of the frame rates is selected wrong... that can be very frustrating." [UNVERIFIED single, 67k] — [ECAMM-v-OBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dxa3BJCyb6Q). Fichter calls OBS "a box of loose wires; you need to tinker." [C: 2 agree OBS needs tinkering — OBSvVMIX, ECAMM-v-OBS; Primal Video also says "you're going to have to go through some level of setup and customization"]
- **OBS has a single scrubber and cannot cue a clip in preview.** To cue a clip on the preview side you "click on it then hit the replay button... it's not intuitive it's confusing they need to put in a second scrub bar." vMix has separate scrubbers for preview and output. [UNVERIFIED, 71k] — [OBSvVMIX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whmgdh79JvE)
- **vMix layers are confusing, but OBS layers win.** OBS has drag-to-reorder sources, groups, an eye toggle and lock, "very similar to Photoshop," with bounding-box handles and alt-drag to crop. vMix multiview layering is "not intuitive... confusing as heck," with no layer grouping or locking. The vMix interface "feels like a commercial production software... usability is not overly intuitive." [UNVERIFIED, 71k]. Primal Video disagrees on the overall workflow: "I'd much prefer the interface and workflow of v-mix than OBS... the way that it handles the different scenes the different cameras." [Conflict: OBSvVMIX vs PRIMAL-PC]
- **OBS audio and sync.** Fixing AV sync needs a millisecond "sync offset" that you find by trial and error ("I usually start by typing in something like 500"). OBS's strength is per-source audio filters such as EQ and compressor, which Ecamm lacks. [UNVERIFIED single, 67k] — [ECAMM-v-OBS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dxa3BJCyb6Q)
- **Prism audio routing.** Game or capture audio added as a source applies "only to that individual scene," while audio added in Settings applies across all scenes. Monitoring creates duplicate audio unless you set "monitor only, mute output." These are routing traps a beginner must learn. [V: 276k] — [PRISM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LxkmWXR6dVs)
- **ATEM hides key modes in a separate app.** Cut-bus vs. program/preview mode lives in "ATEM Setup", not in Software Control: "it should be... part of the main software but it's only in the setup portion." [V: 337k] — [ATEM-KR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C4ERujxrXk4). PhotoJoseph confirms that preview on the Mini works only after switching mode in ATEM Setup, and that the software download is "not in the box... they don't really make it that clear where to get the software." [V: 286k] [C: 2] — [ATEM-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sFH-dT2s02w)
- **ATEM connection failure has no clear recovery.** "No switcher connected" sometimes appears even when cabled. The fix is to unplug and replug the Ethernet, or use File > Connections and set a manual IP. Installing updates needs uninstall-and-reboot cycles. [V: 337k] — [ATEM-KR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C4ERujxrXk4)
- **Tablet switchers: the program audio comes only from the main device.** "The sound that goes into your production is what is in the main production switcher; it does not include the sound from other devices" (Switcher). [V: 142k, vendor]. Mevo instead lets you tap per-camera meters to enable a mic and gives per-camera level control. It warns that monitoring on device speakers causes echo, so headphones are required. [V: 117k, sponsored] [C: 2 on "audio source selection is a key pain point on phone multicam"] — [SW-Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MlobdIgwxj0), [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4)
- **Hidden quality cap.** The Mevo app defaults to **480p**. Higher options are greyed out until you find "increase input quality" in Settings. [V: 117k] — [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4). StreamYard similarly defaults to 720p unless you upgrade or change "broadcast quality" in settings. [V: 135k] [C: 2 on "default quality is silently low"]
- **Restream complaints.** Streams dropped on the free plan and stopped once he paid; "changing layouts... a little cumbersome... slower to change." [UNVERIFIED, 30k] — [RS-Minn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNn6HbDIrlE). Feyrer Jr. calls Restream's pricing "goofy" because you can't remove branding below $50 a month. [UNVERIFIED, 34k]
- **StreamYard's limits.** It has only one extra camera per participant. A guest cannot add an extra camera. There was no native camera-switch hotkey at that time, and the extra camera uses the primary person's mic. [UNVERIFIED, 15k] — [SY-Multicam](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcTGhtR0U9w). Primal Video stopped recommending StreamYard over "some pretty serious issues" (not specified). [UNVERIFIED]
- **Hidden CPU cost.** OBS Studio Mode "does tend to take a bit more processing power... your stream starts to look a little laggy and jumpy." [UNVERIFIED single, 67k] Prism "does use a bit more CPU usage than OBS." [V: 276k] Streamlabs adds 8% or more CPU at idle. [V: 194k] [C: 3 channels agree that convenience layers cost CPU]

### Inferences
- A web app should never show an empty black canvas. Start from preset layouts with sources already placed. Never let a resolution or frame-rate mismatch fail silently: show a clear "no signal / format mismatch" state on the tile.
- Put audio source of truth and routing in one place: a mixer strip per input with an explicit "goes to program" indicator. Avoid per-scene audio, which is Prism's trap.
- Put every mode the show depends on (preview/program, quality) in the main UI, never in a separate setup tool. Default quality to the target (1080p60), not to a safe low setting.

### Gaps
- No high-view video specifically documents vMix audio-bus complaints or Wirecast UX pain. PTZOptics (27k) praised Wirecast's unlimited inputs and ONVIF discovery but said little about UI.
- **Unverified list:** OBS single scrubber (Fichter). vMix layer confusion (Fichter). OBS mismatches cause black screens, and Studio Mode lag (Tom Buck). Restream drops and slow layouts (Minn Media). StreamYard extra-camera limits (Kirk Nugent). StreamYard "serious issues" (Primal Video; a web search found no documented controversy, only undated Trustpilot billing complaints and a 2026 terms consolidation under Bending Spoons — [Trustpilot](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/streamyard.com)).

## Q3. Best patterns: director's multiview with program/preview, one-click cuts, keyboard shortcuts, and auto-director/AI switching (how AI activity is shown and overridden)

### Takeaway
The pro standard has five parts:
- a multiview with program (red) and preview (green) on top and numbered input tiles below, plus audio meters and tally banners
- CUT and AUTO buttons with a T-bar
- clicking a tile to send it to preview
- hotkeys and Stream Deck
- consumer apps that skip preview and cut on tap

Today's "auto-directors" in streaming software are mostly not AI. Mevo has a priority camera with a star and a dwell-time slider. Ecamm, OBS and ATEM use timer or macro loops, and manual selection overrides them. Real AI speaker-switching shows up mostly in post-production (Riverside) and in meeting-room hardware. None of the observed products shows the reasoning behind an auto-switch decision.

### Cited Findings
**Program/preview and multiview conventions**
- In ATEM Software Control, "preview is always green, program is always red." In program/preview mode, CUT swaps preview and program, so pressing CUT repeatedly toggles between two cameras. AUTO runs the chosen transition (mix, dip, wipe) with a set duration. A T-bar gives a manual fade. [V: 286k; C: 2 — ATEM-PJ, ATEM-KR (337k)] — [ATEM-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sFH-dT2s02w), [ATEM-KR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C4ERujxrXk4)
- Multiview is defined as "preview and program on top, up to 8 inputs below." [V: 286k] The vMix multiview has preview and output, then 8 input tiles, with **colored banners for preview/output, audio bars, and input numbers**. It can be set to "click to select input" (tap a tile to send it to preview). It can be sent to a browser "telestrator" on a tablet, with a custom shortcut button ("Fade") to take preview to program. A single-input multiview works as a wireless confidence monitor. [UNVERIFIED vendor, 45k; consistent with ATEM-PJ on layout] — [VMIX-MV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tD--1IMzsJU)
- ATEM can show **camera operator names on multiview tiles** "making it much easier for the TD to know who they're working with". **Fade to Black can also fade audio**, which avoids "embarrassing moments... when you think you've gone offline but the audio is actually still alive." [UNVERIFIED, 55k] — [ATEM-19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iLu4IoB16GE)
- OBS (v26+) and vMix both have a T-bar. OBS Studio Mode shows preview on the left and program on the right. [C: 3 — OBSvVMIX, ECAMM-v-OBS, OBS-Primal (1.6M)] — [OBS-Primal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9z9GiEM4uvA)
- Ecamm has an optional "program mode" with two windows for "that same professional control room switching ability if you want it". The default is a single live preview. [UNVERIFIED, 67k]

**One-tap direct cuts (consumer pattern)**
- Mevo, Switcher and Cinamaker: tap a camera to cut directly to program. Transition type and duration are a global setting (cut, crossfade, slide, edge wipe). Cinamaker allows a **per-camera transition**. [C: 3 — MEVO-PJ (117k), SW-Overview (142k), CINA]
- **Mevo's crop-as-virtual-camera pattern.** A blue box on the wide shot shows the program crop. Tap to cut-zoom to a preset (1.7x). **Tap and hold opens an action menu with "cut / rabbit (fast) / turtle (slow)" moves**, and pinch controls the move live. A setting chooses "auto wide on app disconnect" for shot safety. [V: 117k, sponsored] — [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4). Phones in Mevo Go can be cropped and panned the same way. [V: 325k] — [MEVO-Unlockr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPBdWM7Z158)
- Switcher Studio: **long-press a source and choose "Combine"** to make an instant picture-in-picture or multi-source layout. A yellow outline shows that you are editing a multiview layout, and a red outline shows live. [V: 142k, vendor]

**Keyboard and hardware shortcuts**
- OBS, Ecamm and Prism all support hotkeys per scene and Stream Deck. [C: 3 — ECAMM-v-OBS, PRISM, OBS-Brett (hotkeys section)]. Prism adds a **phone remote-control "deck"** paired by QR code. [V: 276k]. StreamYard has a "Hotkeys" settings area. [C: 1, SY-Gauld]

**Auto-director / automated switching**
- **Mevo Auto Director** is a toggle on the main screen. In settings you **star a priority camera** and set a **slider for how long to stay on each shot** ("switch every... eight seconds"). The reviewer starts it and walks away. [V: 117k, sponsored] — [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4)
- **Ecamm "automagic" switching** is explicitly "not a smart switching situation where it automatically knows when somebody is talking... this is kind of a dumb switching situation." You put scenes in a folder, give each scene an invisible countdown timer set to "go to next scene when finished," and vary the durations (1:30, 0:20, 2:00) so it "feels a little more organic." **Override:** "if you want to take manual control... manually select whatever scene you want... jump right back into your folder at any time and the countdown timers will pick up right where you left off." [UNVERIFIED, 22k] — [ECAMM-Auto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HRtjeAQDks)
- **OBS Advanced Scene Switcher macro:** a random scene group with a random wait of 4–10 seconds between cuts. Clicking a non-auto scene stops it. [UNVERIFIED, 20k] — [OBS-Auto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJX-G0f1T9w). **ATEM macro loop** with pause steps and a loop toggle, started with Run and stopped with Stop. [UNVERIFIED, 39k] — [ATEM-Auto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CsNdWviUeX4). [C: 3 channels agree that the common "auto switch" is timer or rotation based, with manual selection as the override]
- **AI speaker-aware switching** mostly appears in post-production. Riverside "Smart Scenes" sets layouts (active speaker full screen, grid, split) on a strip above the timeline, and "Magic Mute" hatches out silent sections. [UNVERIFIED vendor, 10k] — [RIV-Smart](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oJCI4_YDYa8). Meeting and production hardware does it live: Datavideo VoiceTRX50 cuts to the PTZ camera assigned to the active microphone, and Crestron Automate VX uses face-angle visual AI — [Datavideo](https://datavideo.com/br/article/645/capture-whoever-speaks-datavideo-voicetrx-delivers-intelligent-voice-activated-production), [UC Today](https://www.uctoday.com/devices-workspace-tech/crestron-adds-new-ai-to-its-automate-vx-to-enable-multi-camera-switching-in-meetings/) (web, not YouTube).
- Riverside's AI edit can be fully overridden: "you can change any of these edits after the fact in case AI gets it wrong." [UNVERIFIED, 55k] — [RIV-Red11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UlJC5MWd5Uw)

### Inferences
- **Design for Sunrise Jam:**
  - A multiview with program (red) on top and preview (green) beside it, with numbered phone tiles below. Each tile carries an operator or phone name, an audio meter, and tally borders.
  - Click a tile to send it to preview. Space or Enter for CUT, a key for AUTO, and number keys 1–9 to preview.
  - An optional "direct mode" where a tap cuts straight to program, which is the consumer default.
- **AI director controls.** Copy Mevo's simple controls (on/off, priority camera, min/max dwell slider) and Ecamm's override rule (any manual cut pauses or resumes the auto director, which picks up where it left off). Go further than the market by showing the AI's *next intended shot in the preview bus* and its *reason* ("cutting to Keys: solo detected"). That makes preview the natural place for the AI to propose cuts and for the human to veto them. No product observed does this, so it is a differentiator, not a proven pattern.
- Add a Fade to Black that also fades audio, as a safety control.

### Gaps
- No YouTube video was found showing live AI speaker- or music-aware auto-switching in consumer streaming software with a visible AI-state UI. This is a market gap rather than missing evidence.
- No 100k-plus video covered TriCaster multiview customization. NewTek's own multiviewer videos have under 10k views.
- **Unverified list:** vMix clickable and tablet multiview (vendor). ATEM operator names and audio fade-to-black (Doug Johnson). Ecamm timer auto-switch. OBS ASS random switching. ATEM macro loop. Riverside Smart Scenes. Ecamm program mode.

## Q4. Phone-camera onboarding, guest joining, and health indicators

### Takeaway
The easiest onboarding has these features:
- a link or QR code that opens in the phone browser with no account (StreamYard, VDO.Ninja) or a free companion app on the same Wi-Fi (Mevo Go, Switcher, Cinamaker)
- the phone appears automatically in the source tray
- device permission prompts on the phone
- the choice of camera, audio, or screencast role on the phone
- a lock button and naming on the phone side

Health shows up as per-device Wi-Fi, battery and storage indicators (Mevo), a bitrate disclosure, a "stream health" tab (YouTube), and Wi-Fi optimization modes (Switcher).

### Cited Findings
**Join flows**
- StreamYard guests: copy the invite link, and the guest "don't even need to create account." Guests land in a **backstage** area and the host clicks "add to stage." Guests see "you're in the show — everyone can see and hear you." [V: 135k; C: 3 — SY-Think, SY-Gauld, SY-Multicam] — [SY-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDx8fKnU3Sk). The guest page asks for camera and mic permission, a display name and an audio check, then shows "enter studio." [C: 2 — SYvRS for both StreamYard and Restream]
- VDO.Ninja ("create reusable invite", set max resolution): **scan the QR code with the phone camera**, tap "share camera", allow permissions, choose the rear camera and no audio, turn the phone sideways and press start. Then paste the browser-source link into OBS. [V: 427k] — [VDO-Curry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86S0tV1QeCE). The same flow is confirmed with the advice that the same network means lower latency, and an Ethernet adapter gives the best reliability. [V: 77k] [C: 2] — [VDO-LPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmcrEvXxmlE)
- Mevo Go: no login needed ("grab somebody's phone, install the app and as long as you're on the same network"). The phone chooses a role: **video+audio / screencast / remote microphone only**. The controller taps "+", sees discovered devices and taps Connect. Phones have a **lock button** (hold to unlock) so bystanders can't change settings, plus a name and password. Focus and exposure locks are available. "This app has to be loaded and active... you can't take a phone call." Power is recommended. A **warning appears when adding cameras that load Wi-Fi**. Android and iOS can be mixed. [C: 2 — MEVO-Lon (43k, sponsored), MEVO-Unlockr (325k)] — [MEVO-Lon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ZCpRhIPflY), [MEVO-Unlockr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPBdWM7Z158). One phone can act as a hotspot for outdoor shoots, and devices re-discover automatically. [V: 325k]
- Switcher Studio: launch the app on each device on the same Wi-Fi, and devices "appear in the list." Tap to add (blue check) or remove. The director can **remotely control zoom, focus and exposure and lock them**. [V: 142k, vendor]. Cinamaker uses the same pattern (Capture app on phones, director app on iPad), with remote ISO, white balance and focus, and it detects an external mic on a phone automatically ("it knows it's external audio"). [UNVERIFIED, 20k] [C: 3 on "same-Wi-Fi auto-discovery plus remote camera control"]
- The StreamYard phone-as-host flow works in the mobile browser with "no app": choose the back camera in settings, mirror it, use banners and comments. The vendor's tips are **strong signal, a tripod, plug the phone in, and a quiet space**. [UNVERIFIED vendor, 55k; the battery advice matches MEVO-Lon] — [SY-Phone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-UqUffUWEoo)

**Health indicators**
- The Mevo per-camera menu shows **Wi-Fi status, battery status and free storage**. A disclosure triangle shows **input bitrate and free device space**. There is a **latency setting (low/standard/high)** to trade delay for robustness on busy Wi-Fi. [V: 117k] — [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4). TheUnlockr confirms latency and quality settings. [C: 2]
- Switcher's **"Wi-Fi camera communication: Optimize for bad Wi-Fi"** setting buffers video to stop jumpy cameras. "Director mode" records 4K on each device while sending a lighter live feed. [V: 142k, vendor]
- YouTube Live Control Room has a **"Stream health" tab** ("real-time status report") and an analytics tab (concurrent viewers, chat rate). [V: 1.5M, vendor] — [YT-LCR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAo2gN3K3GQ). Prism shows "stream and video health information" in the bottom bar with an up-arrow for details. [V: 276k] [C: 2]
- OBS 24+ has **dynamic bitrate**: "instead of dropping frames... dynamically changes the bitrate on the fly". The tradeoff is more delay, and it is not compatible with low-latency mode. [V: 198k] — [OBS-Bitrate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MVwyFNZzxic)
- Cameras that switch to stealth LEDs once connected avoid distracting performers on stage. [V: 117k] — [MEVO-PJ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqGbyMnrpG4)

### Inferences
- **Sunrise Jam phone onboarding:**
  - A QR code on the director screen opens the iPhone web page (or deep-links to the Android app) with no login.
  - The phone shows one big role choice (Camera / Camera+Mic / Mic only), permission prompts, a name field (default "Cam 3 – Drums"), and a lock button.
  - The phone shows a "you're LIVE" tally from the director.
  - On the director side, the new phone appears in a backstage tray showing battery %, charging state, Wi-Fi/RSSI, bitrate, fps, thermal state and latency, with Accept/Reject.
- Health colors should sit directly on each tile, with numbers one click away (Prism's up-arrow and Mevo's disclosure triangle). Pair them with an automatic fallback (dynamic bitrate) that is *announced* in the process sidebar.

### Gaps
- No high-view video showed per-phone battery or thermal warnings during a live show. Mevo shows battery in a settings menu, not as an alert. Battery alerts are therefore an inferred need backed by advice ("plug your phone in") rather than an observed UI.
- **Unverified list:** Cinamaker external-mic detection. StreamYard mobile tips (vendor). Switcher bad-Wi-Fi mode and director mode (vendor, but 142k views, so it counts as V).

## Q5. Go-live checklist / pre-flight UX, break screens with countdowns, error states, recovery

### Takeaway
The good go-live flows have:
- a scheduled "event" with destination, title, privacy and thumbnail set before the show, then an "Enter studio" step with a device check
- a final confirmation dialog that re-shows quality and recording options
- a visible LIVE badge with an elapsed timer and viewer count
- a two-step End
- a private or unlisted test stream as the standard rehearsal

Break and countdown screens are pre-loaded clips or typed full-screen graphics toggled with one tap. Timed lower thirds that hide themselves reduce operator load.

### Cited Findings
**Pre-flight and go-live**
- StreamYard: create broadcast → destinations → title, description and privacy (public, unlisted, private) → schedule → **enter studio with a device check (mute, camera, settings: camera, resolution, mic, speakers, echo cancellation)**. Before going live, **"double check the edit section"** for destination, title and thumbnail. Clicking Go Live shows a **confirmation prompt that repeats broadcast quality (1080p) and local-recording toggles**. Once live there is a **"LIVE" badge and elapsed timer** top-left. **End broadcast needs a second confirmation** "so you don't accidentally end it." [V: 135k] — [SY-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDx8fKnU3Sk). Stewart Gauld confirms a scheduled-stream pre-check and live view count. [C: 2]
- YouTube: a **private stream "is great for testing your live stream to make sure all of the tech is functioning properly."** [V: 414k] — [YT-Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6cVEfihXNnk). In Live Control Room, a watch page and notifications don't happen "until you actually go live from your encoder." Previous stream settings are copied over, and there is "stream setup help." [V: 1.5M] — [YT-LCR](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAo2gN3K3GQ). Tom Buck uses a "private unlisted test stream" to tune AV sync. [C: 2+]
- Riverside: Go Live starts a **countdown to zero**, then records locally while streaming. A live/record menu shows **per-platform viewer counts and links to check each destination**. [UNVERIFIED vendor, 26k] — [RIV-Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTJmCT3wKAQ)
- Switcher: after Stop, **"don't dismiss this dialogue — let your video finish transferring to the server."** This is an explicit post-show state. [V: 142k, vendor]
- Mevo: the hardware button's last-config stream "will simply fail if it can't stream", and the default action is set to record. Firmware updates are prompted per camera at setup. [V: 117k]
- Beginner first-stream advice: expect mistakes, and use a test-fail-learn loop. "Your follow alerts somehow aren't working... transitions" are typical first-stream failures. Ask viewers how the mic and game audio sound. [V: 115k] — [TIPS-Cpaws](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9E5ERUzfHfA)
- OBS added an in-app YouTube control panel and chat docks "so you can monitor things while you're live." [V: 1.6M] — [OBS-Primal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9z9GiEM4uvA)

**Break and "we'll be back" screens and countdowns**
- StreamYard and Restream ship **countdown video clips** in the Video clips / Overlay section, which you play on hover with Show. [C: 3 — SY-Think, SY-Gauld, SYvRS] Restream offers "a countdown timer, say if you're going live and you want to show this before you go" and a scene for "a pre-show countdown." [C: 2 — RS-Minn, RS-2.0]
- Mevo: "full screen overlay" text on a color or image "to do maybe a be right back screen... tap on and off from inside the graphics bin." [V: 325k] — [MEVO-Unlockr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nPBdWM7Z158)
- Prism has a clock widget for countdown or time of day. [V: 276k]
- Ecamm's countdown timer overlay can auto-advance to the next scene, and an outro clip can **end the broadcast automatically when it finishes**. [UNVERIFIED, 22k]
- Riverside lower thirds and on-screen comments can carry a **timer (5/10/20 s) so they hide themselves**: "I don't even have to think about hiding it manually." [UNVERIFIED vendor, 26k]

**Recovery and resilience**
- Local or ISO recording per participant is the main resilience feature. Riverside records locally and uploads, so "internet issues shouldn't break the quality," and it also keeps a stream backup. [V: 55k + StreamYard local recording in SY-Think 135k, SY-Gauld] [C: 3]
- StreamYard on a weak mobile signal: "it won't turn off your stream but it will interrupt your stream and you might be a little pixelated." [UNVERIFIED vendor]
- OBS dynamic bitrate degrades quality rather than dropping frames. [V: 198k]
- Mevo can auto-zoom wide on app disconnect. [V: 117k]

### Inferences
- **Pre-flight for Sunrise Jam:** a one-screen checklist that turns green item by item:
  - YouTube auth and event, title, privacy and thumbnail
  - 1080p60 output confirmed
  - each phone connected, with battery at least 50% or charging and Wi-Fi OK
  - mixer channels receiving signal, with a peak check
  - AI director and auto-mixer state
  - local recording on
  - an optional "private test stream" button

  Go Live shows a confirmation that restates the key settings, then a 5-4-3-2-1 countdown that plays the "Starting soon" scene. While live, keep a persistent LIVE badge, elapsed time, viewers and stream health. End needs two steps and offers an "Ending / thanks" scene first.
- **Break screen:** one big "BRB" button that cuts to a branded slate with an editable countdown (for example 10:00), ducks or switches audio to a break playlist or a room mic, and optionally auto-returns to the last camera layout at zero. Combine Ecamm's auto-advance with Mevo and StreamYard's one-tap simplicity. The ATEM-style fade-to-black-with-audio prevents a hot mic during breaks.

### Gaps
- No high-view video showed a formal pre-flight checklist UI inside a streaming tool. The pattern is assembled from fragments: device check, confirmation dialog, test streams.
- **Unverified list:** Riverside countdown and auto-hide timers (vendor). Ecamm auto-end via outro clip. StreamYard mobile signal behavior (vendor).

## Cross-cutting: how pro features are hidden until needed (progressive disclosure)

### Takeaway
Every "easy" tool has a power layer, but keeps it off by default:
- Studio Mode / program mode is an opt-in toggle (OBS, Prism, Ecamm)
- the advanced output settings sit behind a "simple" mode (OBS, Prism)
- Ecamm's dockable panels can be closed
- StreamYard's custom layout editor is behind an "edit" button
- Mevo's crop speeds, auto-director priorities and latency live in app settings while tap-to-cut is the main UI
- ISO and director-mode recording is a toggle in output settings (Switcher)

### Cited Findings
- Studio Mode is off by default and should be "toggled off" for beginners. [V: 1.6M + 276k; C: 2 — OBS-Primal, PRISM]
- OBS Output "simple mode... will work for most of you"; the Advanced settings are "default should work just fine". [V: 6.3M] — [OBS-Brett](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTk99mHDX_I). Prism: "for the output mode keep this at simple." [V: 276k] [C: 2]
- StreamYard's layout "edit" (custom layouts) sits behind a button the tutorial skips ("let's leave that for now"). [UNVERIFIED, 76k sponsored]
- Mevo app-wide settings hold crop speeds, the action-menu delay, latency and auto-director priorities. The main UI is just camera tiles plus record/live. [V: 117k]
- Switcher puts director mode and per-camera ISO recording in Outputs > advanced. [V: 142k vendor]
- Primal Video's selection advice: pick "one that is easy for you to use that's not overwhelming with a bunch of extra settings... unless you need that." Evmux "can really scale with you." [C: 1 channel, 2 videos]

### Inferences
- For a web app: default to "Show mode", meaning a stage, phone tiles, a few big buttons (Go Live, BRB, Cut/Auto, AI Director toggle) and a simple fader bank. An explicit "Pro" toggle reveals:
  - the full multiview with preview/program and T-bar
  - per-channel EQ, compressor and bus routing for the 16-ch mixer
  - transitions and macros
  - encoder settings
- Background processes (AI director decisions, auto-mixer moves, dynamic bitrate changes, phone reconnects, recording uploads) belong in the collapsible sidebar as a live activity log. None of the reviewed products has such a log. YouTube's stream health tab and Prism's health bar are the closest.

### Gaps
- No video measured how users discover hidden features. The evidence is qualitative from tutorials only.
