Meet AI Copilot

A private meeting copilot. Reads Google Meet's live captions locally and drafts Claude-powered answers when a question is aimed at you.

Consent & recording laws. Transcribing a conversation is regulated in many places (two-party-consent US states, GDPR in the EU, and elsewhere). This tool captures locally on your machine, but it's on you to get consent where it's required. It doesn't join the call as a bot and adds nothing the other participants can see.
